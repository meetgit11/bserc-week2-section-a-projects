# Official form references — Form Photo Fixer

The form presets in Form Photo Fixer are based on the published requirements of real forms, checked on 5 Nov 2026. **Read this before submitting a real form: rules change every cycle, and the form's own current notice always wins.** Nothing here is officially verified by BSERC or by the issuing authorities, and no preset in the app is claimed to be officially verified.

## 1. JEE Main — National Testing Agency (NTA)

- **Official portal:** jeemain.nta.nic.in (NTA Information Bulletin for the session you are applying in)
- **Photograph:** 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm, JPEG/JPG, **10–200 KB**. Recent colour passport-size photo, plain white or off-white background, face clearly visible (about 80% of the frame), no cap or sunglasses.
- **Signature:** 3.5 cm × 1.5 cm, JPEG/JPG. **2026 bulletin: 4–30 KB.** Reports for the **2027 session give the signature as 10–100 KB** — the limit changed between sessions, so check your session's bulletin.
- **Preset in the app:** photo 350×450 px, 10–200 KB; signature 350×150 px, 4–30 KB (2026 values). The app itself carries a warning about the 2027 signature change.
- **Why it is in the app:** the 2026 photo size (10–200 KB, 3.5×4.5 cm) is consistent across the NTA bulletin and independent summaries of it.

## 2. Indian Passport — Passport Seva (Ministry of External Affairs)

- **Official portal:** passportindia.gov.in (Passport Seva Programme 2.0; "Guidelines for ICAO Compliant Photographs for Passport Applications")
- **Photograph (digital upload):** 35 × 45 mm (3.5 × 4.5 cm), **exactly 630 × 810 px**, JPEG, **10–250 KB**. Plain white background, face occupying 80–85% of the frame, no eyeglasses.
- **Preset in the app:** photo 630×810 px, 10–250 KB; no signature (the passport application does not take a signature image in this flow).
- **Important — do not confuse this with the square format:** the 2 × 2 inch (51 × 51 mm) square photo is for OCI cards and e-Visas, **not** for the Indian passport. Since the September 2025 ICAO standardisation, submitting a square photo for a passport is rejected on upload.

## 3. OCI card / Indian e-Visa (square)

- **Official portals:** ociservices.gov.in (OCI), indianvisaonline.gov.in (e-Visa)
- **Photograph:** 51 × 51 mm (2 × 2 inches), square, **600 × 600 px**, JPEG, **10–300 KB** (e-Visa uploads are also reported as 10 KB–1 MB in some sources; the app uses the stricter 10–300 KB bound). Plain white or off-white background.
- **Preset in the app:** photo 600×600 px, 10–300 KB; no signature.

## 4. Exam Form A — 50 KB limit (example from the Week 2 PDF)

- **Source:** the preview image in the Week 2 project PDF itself (Section A, Form Photo Fixer page), which shows a form needing a 3.5 × 4.5 cm photo with a 50 KB limit ("39 KB of 50 KB limit ✓").
- **Preset in the app:** photo 350×450 px, 10–50 KB; signature 350×150 px, 4–30 KB.
- **Note:** this is the PDF's example, not a named real examination — it is included as a fourth, generic preset for forms with a 50 KB cap. If your form is a named examination, use its own preset (or a custom one) instead.

## What is NOT a preset — and why

- **UPSC (Civil Services / OTR):** deliberately **not** used as a preset. Published UPSC photo/signature specs conflict across sources and years (photo reported as 200×230 px and as 350×350–1000×1000 px; file size as 20–200 KB and as 20–300 KB; signature as 10–50 KB, 20–100 KB and 20–300 KB). Rather than pick one conflicting version, the app leaves UPSC out — if you need it, create a custom preset from the notification for your own cycle and verify it there.

## How to check a form yourself

1. Download the **current** information bulletin / notification for your form and cycle from the official portal above (not from a coaching site).
2. Find the photograph/signature upload section and note: pixel dimensions, physical size, file format, minimum and maximum KB.
3. In Form Photo Fixer, pick the matching preset — or create a custom preset with those exact numbers.
4. After processing, check the downloaded file's properties on your computer (dimensions + KB) before uploading it to the form.
5. If the form's notice and this document disagree, **the notice wins**.
