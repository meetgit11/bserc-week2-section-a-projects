Before you submit: form rules change every cycle. Always re-check the current notice for your form — the preset values here are from the sources below, checked 5 Nov 2026, and the form's own instructions win if they differ.

Sources for the presets

  • JEE Main (NTA): NTA Information Bulletin — photo JPEG 10–200 KB, 3.5 × 4.5 cm; signature JPEG 4–30 KB, 3.5 × 1.5 cm (2026 bulletin). Reports for the 2027 session give the signature as 10–100 KB — check your session's bulletin. Official portal: jeemain.nta.nic.in
  • Indian Passport (Passport Seva 2.0): Passport Seva / Ministry of External Affairs ICAO spec — exactly 630 × 810 px (35 × 45 mm), JPEG 10–250 KB. The 2 × 2 in (51 × 51 mm) square is for OCI cards and e-Visas, not the passport. Official portal: passportindia.gov.in
  • OCI / Indian e-Visa: 51 × 51 mm square, 600 × 600 px, JPEG 10–300 KB. Official portals: ociservices.gov.in / indianvisaonline.gov.in
  • Exam Form A (50 KB): the example shown in the Week 2 project PDF preview — 3.5 × 4.5 cm, 50 KB limit.
